Album Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026 · Last updated: July 22, 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Album Box is a local private album vault. It has no account server, no cloud storage, no advertising SDK, and no third-party analytics SDK.
  • Your imported photos and videos stay on your device unless you choose to sync selected content to a trusted nearby device.
  • Album Box has no remote server and does not transmit any data to any server; outside user-initiated sync, it does not need network access.
  • The app does not collect IDFA, IDFV, location, contacts, microphone data, remote crash logs, or cross-app tracking data.
