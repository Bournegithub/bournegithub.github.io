Album Box Support

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Album Box is a local private photo vault and nearby-device sync tool for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It does not provide a developer-operated account or cloud album service.

What to include in a report

The following details help us understand the issue:

  • Device type: iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  • System version and Album Box version.
  • Steps that led to the problem and the exact error message.
  • An optional screenshot with private photos, videos, passwords, and Apple Account details hidden.

Never email payment-card details, your Apple Account password, device passcode, Album Box password, or original private album files.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my Album Box password or biometrics are unavailable. What can I do?

Try your remaining PIN or pattern and check Face ID or Touch ID in system settings. Album Box has no account server, recovery key, or developer backdoor. If every unlock method is unavailable, clearing app data or reinstalling may be the only way to use the app again, and doing so permanently removes local album data. Support cannot recover or reset the password by email.

How do I import, export, or delete photos and videos?

On iPhone and iPad, open a target folder and use More > Import Photos/Videos. Select items and use Download to export them to Photos. On Mac, use Import or Command + O, and use Download to choose an export folder. Delete an item from its menu, selection actions, or playback page and confirm. Deleting a folder also deletes its subfolders and media; deleted local content cannot be recovered in Album Box.

Which network permissions does nearby sync need?

The current version does not require Bluetooth permission. Nearby sync requires Local Network access and Wi-Fi/WLAN enabled on both devices; keep the devices nearby and on the same local network or available peer-to-peer Wi-Fi path. Album transfers do not use cellular data. App Store purchase or restore actions may use Wi-Fi or cellular data, but they do not require Album Box Local Network permission.

Why can’t sync find the other device, or why did a transfer fail?

Open Device Sync on both devices, keep both screens active, confirm Local Network and Wi-Fi/WLAN access, and make sure both devices use the same Real or Mist space. Avoid VPNs, client-isolated guest networks, and unstable hotspots. Scan the current QR code or enter the current 20-digit code again, reopen the sync page, and retry. Keep devices nearby until transfer completes.

What happens if I delete data, uninstall Album Box, or lose the device?

Album Box stores albums locally. Deleting media, clearing data, uninstalling the app, device damage, or device loss may permanently remove the only copy. Before those actions, export important files or sync them to another trusted device and confirm the copy can be opened. Canceling a subscription does not delete local photos or videos.

Can the developer restore my albums from the cloud?

No. Album Box has no developer-operated cloud backup or account server, and the developer never receives your album contents. Support cannot retrieve local photos, videos, passwords, or encryption credentials from a server.

Subscription help

Apple processes purchases, purchase restoration, and entitlement verification through StoreKit.

  • In Album Box, open the subscription or subscription-status page and choose Restore Purchases.
  • Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the App Store, and restore on each supported device as needed.
  • Manage or turn off auto-renewal on Apple’s subscription management page.
  • Send billing disputes and refund requests through Apple’s Report a Problem page.
  • The developer does not receive payment-card details and cannot approve an Apple refund directly.
  • Canceling auto-renewal does not automatically delete local photos or videos in Album Box.
